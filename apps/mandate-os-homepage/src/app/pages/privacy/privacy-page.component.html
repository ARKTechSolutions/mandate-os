Privacy Policy

This page explains how the MandateOS marketing website (getmandateos.com) uses cookies and analytics. It is provided for transparency and should be reviewed by counsel for your jurisdiction.

Who we are

MandateOS is operated by ARK Tech Solutions. This privacy notice covers the public marketing website only. The product application at app.getmandateos.com may have additional terms and processing activities.

What we collect

When you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-F1FFV1W68C) to understand how visitors navigate the site and to measure marketing campaigns, including traffic from ads. Typical data includes pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP (with IP anonymization enabled), device/browser information, referral source, and campaign parameters such as UTM tags.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors and sessions. These cookies are not set if you reject analytics cookies. Necessary cookies required for basic site operation are not used for advertising profiling on this marketing site.

Legal basis

Where consent is required (for example under the GDPR and ePrivacy rules in the EU/EEA/UK), we process analytics data only after you choose Accept on the cookie banner. You may reject analytics and still use the website.

How long we keep data

Google Analytics retention follows the retention period configured in our GA4 property (commonly 2 or 14 months for event-level data). Aggregated reports may be kept longer.

Your choices

You can Accept or Reject analytics cookies via the banner shown on first visit. To change your choice later, use in the site footer, or follow the Cookie preferences control on any page. Rejecting (or withdrawing) consent stops new analytics cookies from being used for measurement.

International transfers

Google LLC processes Analytics data and may transfer it outside your country, including to the United States. Google participates in frameworks such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. See Google’s documentation for details on Analytics data processing.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website, contact us through the channels listed on GitHub or your usual MandateOS contact.
